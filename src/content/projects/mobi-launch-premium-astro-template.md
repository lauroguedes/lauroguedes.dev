Mobi Launch is a paid Astro template for presenting an app before or during its launch. It gives the product a focused page for explaining the idea, showing the interface, answering common questions, and collecting interest through a waitlist.

I designed it for developers and small product teams that already have an application but do not want to assemble a launch site from unrelated marketing sections. The template includes the source and is meant to be customized, hosted, and extended as part of the buyer's own project.

## A launch page with a clear job

The page starts with the product message and a direct signup action. The rest of the layout explains what the visitor gets, how the product moves from idea to launch, and what they should know before joining the list.

The default structure includes:

- a hero with product copy, launch notice, and waitlist form
- desktop and mobile product previews
- sections for benefits, branding, and launch steps
- a question-and-answer section
- repeated signup actions at natural decision points
- a compact closing section and creator credit

The demo content is written for an app launch, but the copy and imagery are examples. Buyers replace them with their own product, screenshots, signup endpoint, and domain.

## Responsive product presentation

Animated phone previews are the main visual device. They show how an app can be presented in both light and dark contexts without relying on a large gallery or video. The desktop layout gives those devices room to overlap, while the mobile layout reorganizes the same story for a narrow screen.

The design uses a dark green base, warm off-white surfaces, and a terracotta accent. Light and dark themes have been designed as a pair rather than generated by simply reversing colors. Theme controls, navigation, forms, and product cards keep the same hierarchy in both modes.

## Source that is meant to change

Mobi Launch is built with Astro, React, Tailwind CSS, and TypeScript. Focused configuration files hold the main content, palette, screenshots, and motion settings, so a first customization does not require rewriting the component tree.

The buyer receives the editable source. The output is a static site that can run anywhere static files are served. Hosting, the domain, and the email signup service remain separate, which leaves those operational choices with the project owner.

The template is not a hosted website builder. A basic development workflow is required to install dependencies, replace assets, connect the waitlist, build the site, and deploy it.

## Commercial scope

Mobi Launch is a premium paid template. Joining the waitlist shown in the demo does not purchase the template or grant a license.

Each purchase covers one customized end product for the buyer or one client. Reselling the template itself or redistributing its source is not included. The commercial license supplied with the purchase contains the complete terms.

## What I wanted the template to solve

A launch page often starts late, after most of the product work is already finished. That usually leads to a generic page or a rushed collection of sections that do not explain the product well.

Mobi Launch provides a coherent starting point while leaving the important decisions editable. The design, responsive behavior, themes, and interaction patterns are already in place. The buyer can spend the remaining time on the message, product imagery, and the signup path that matter for the launch.
